Privacy Policy

OmniShop POS · Last updated: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Omnishop POS ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use the OmniShop POS application, the OmniShop PC app and our online service (together, the "Service").

1. Information we collect

  • Owner account: username, password (stored only as a one-way encrypted hash), phone number with country code, email address (optional) and country.
  • Employee accounts: usernames, password hashes and permissions that an owner creates for their staff.
  • Business data you enter: business names, stock and prices, purchase and selling costs, sales, derived stock recipes, items in use, expenses, and debts and leases (including the names of customers or suppliers you record).
  • Subscription and payment information: your plan, billing cycle, payment status and dates. M-Pesa payments use the phone number you provide for the payment prompt. Card and mobile-money payments by card are handled by Paystack; we never see or store your card number.
  • Account recovery: your registered phone number and email, and the names of one business and one employee, are used only to confirm that you own the account if you forget your password.
  • Device information: a random device ID created by the app and technical details of the phone or computer (such as phone model, screen size, processor, graphics chip and browser). We use them only to recognise approved devices and to stop unapproved devices from opening an account.
  • Usage information: the time you were last active and your country, used to run and support the Service.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service, including syncing your data between your devices and working offline.
  • To manage subscriptions, trials, plan limits and payments.
  • To send low-stock and payment reminders inside the app and as phone notifications.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and provide support.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your data and we do not use your business data for advertising.

3. Where your data is stored

Data is stored on your device (in a local database so the app works without internet) and on our servers, which are hosted with Render in Frankfurt, Germany. This means your information may be transferred outside your country. We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), for these transfers.

4. Who can see your data

  • You (the owner) can see all data in your businesses.
  • Your employees can see only the business they belong to, and only the features you allow.
  • OmniShop administrators can see account-level details only: username, country, plan, number of businesses and employees, business names, activity status and suspension status. They cannot see your stock, sales, expenses, debts, statistics, phone number or email through the administration tools.
  • Service providers that process data for us: Render (hosting), Safaricom M-Pesa and Paystack (payments).
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

New devices must be approved by the account owner before they can log in. Owners can see and remove their devices at any time; removing a device logs it out and erases the data saved on it.

5. Information about your customers

If you record customers, suppliers or staff in the app, you are responsible for having a lawful reason to do so. We process that information only on your behalf to run the Service.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your data within a reasonable period, except records we must keep by law (for example payment records).

7. Security

Passwords are hashed, connections are encrypted, access is limited by role, and login attempts are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your device locked.

8. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and similar laws, you may ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, or object to certain processing. Contact us using the details below. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya or the authority in your country.

9. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not intended for people under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will show the new date above and, for important changes, notify you in the app.
